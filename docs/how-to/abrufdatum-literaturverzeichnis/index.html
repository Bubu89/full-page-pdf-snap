Retrieval date and bibliography: citing web sources, measured rather than asserted

Why a reference list carries a retrieval date, how much a tool takes off your hands when you build the bibliography, and where it hands the work back — summarised from this site's citation measurements. Every figure carries its date and links to its raw data.

As of 23 October 2026 · German-first summary · measurements from 2 to 18 August 2026

A private, non-commercial account — not legal advice and not guidance from a university. Which citation style applies and how the retrieval date is written is set by your department; its rules come before anything here. Disclosure: the author develops Full Page PDF Snap, which appears below as one of several options.

1. What the retrieval date is for

A web source is not a book: it can be edited, moved or deleted after you cite it. The retrieval date says this is what stood there on that day. How often that matters shows in a sample from 2 August 2026: 150 sources from the references of German-language Wikipedia articles on economic and social policy, at most one per server.

FindingShare
The server reports the page as deleted (HTTP 404 or 410)19.3 % (29/150)
No copy in the Internet Archive8.7 % (13)
Deleted and never archived — unrecoverable2.0 % (3)
Age of the newest archived copy, median603 days

Wikipedia references are maintained, so these values err on the kind side. And where an archived copy exists, it is a median of 603 days old — often not the version you read. A date alone does not make a claim checkable; that takes a copy. The consequence: secure the source at the moment you cite it, not at the end of the work. measurement · raw data

2. How the retrieval date appears in the entry

The citation style sets the form — APA, Harvard, DIN 1505-2 and ISO 690 differ in it, and German entries join it with „abgerufen am“. Full Page PDF Snap puts a file ending in .zitate.txt beside every PDF: one finished line each in APA 7, MLA 9, Chicago, Harvard, DIN 1505-2 and ISO 690, plus BibTeX and an RIS record for Citavi, Zotero and EndNote. In the RIS record the retrieval date is written in the form Citavi and EndNote import, with the time — pages change in the course of a day: 

Y2  - 2026/08/18/11:00

If the page gives no year of publication, the entry says „o. J.“ (n.d.), not the year of retrieval. An invented year costs more than a gap that shows. measurement · zitate.js

5. What stays with you

  • Read the completeness flag, not the title. A statistics portal returned „Forschung, Innovation, Digitalisierung“ with STATISTIK AUSTRIA as author — it looked finished and was marked incomplete.
  • Pages behind a login you have access to: open them in your own browser and save them there. A server outside cannot read them; an extension in your session can.
  • Newspaper articles, statistics pages and grey literature: describe them yourself. These pages answered in full and still did not declare what a reference needs — sometimes the author was missing, sometimes the year.
  • Keep the copy that goes with the retrieval date — as a PDF, or for public pages via Save Page Now at the Internet Archive, which creates a dated link any reader can check.

Eine Internetquelle ist kein Buch: Sie kann nach dem Zitieren geändert, verschoben oder gelöscht werden. Das Abrufdatum sagt, das stand an diesem Tag dort.   • Das Kennzeichen „vollständig“ lesen, nicht den Titel. Ein Statistikportal lieferte „Forschung, Innovation, Digitalisierung“ mit STATISTIK AUSTRIA als Verfasser — es sah fertig aus und war als unvollständig markiert.

2. Come compare la data di consultazione nella voce

La forma la stabilisce lo stile di citazione — APA, Harvard, DIN 1505-2 e ISO 690 differiscono in questo, e le voci in tedesco la introducono con „abgerufen am“. 5. Что остаётся за вами

  • Смотреть на флаг полноты, а не на заглавие. Статистический портал вернул „Forschung, Innovation, Digitalisierung“ с автором STATISTIK AUSTRIA — выглядело готовым и было помечено как неполное.
  • Страницы за входом, к которому у вас есть доступ: откройте их в своём браузере и сохраните там. Внешний сервер их прочитать не может, расширение в вашем сеансе — может.
  • Газетные статьи, страницы статистики и «серую» литературу описывайте сами: эти страницы отвечали полностью и всё же не сообщали того, что нужно для записи: где-то не было автора, где-то года.
  • Хранить копию, соответствующую дате обращения — в виде PDF или, для открытых страниц, через Save Page Now в Internet Archive, который создаёт датированную ссылку, доступную для проверки любому читателю.

1. 访问日期有什么用

网络来源不是书：被引用之后，它可能被修改、移动或删除。访问日期表明那一天那里写的是这些。它有多重要，2026 年 8 月 2 日的一份样本可以说明：从德语维基百科经济与社会政策条目的参考资料中抽取 150 个来源，每个服务器最多一个。

2. 访问日期在条目中如何呈现

格式由引用规范决定 —— APA、Harvard、DIN 1505-2 和 ISO 690 各不相同，德语条目用„abgerufen am“连接。Full Page PDF Snap 会在每个 PDF 旁边放一个以 .zitate.txt 结尾的文件：APA 7、MLA 9、Chicago、Harvard、DIN 1505-2 和 ISO 690 各一行现成条目，另附 BibTeX 以及供 Citavi、Zotero 和 EndNote 使用的 RIS 记录。在 RIS 记录中，访问日期按 Citavi 和 EndNote 能导入的格式书写，并带有时间 —— 页面在一天之内也会变化： 

Y2  - 2026/08/18/11:00

如果页面没有给出出版年份，条目中写的是„o. J.“（无年份），而不是访问的年份。编造的年份比一眼可见的空缺代价更高。 测量 · zitate.js

5. 仍需你自己完成的事

  • 看“完整”标记，而不是看标题。一个统计门户以 STATISTIK AUSTRIA 为作者返回了„Forschung, Innovation, Digitalisierung“ —— 看上去已经完成，实际被标为不完整。
  • 你有权访问的登录后页面：在你自己的浏览器中打开并在那里保存。外部服务器读不到它们，而你会话中的扩展可以。
  • 报纸文章、统计页面和灰色文献：自己填写著录信息。这些页面完整地作出了响应，却没有声明一条记录所需的信息：有的缺作者，有的缺年份。
  • 保存与访问日期对应的副本 —— 保存为 PDF；如果是公开页面，也可以用 Internet Archive 的 Save Page Now，它会生成任何读者都能核查的带日期链接。

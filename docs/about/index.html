How the figures are produced

  • Every figure carries the date it was obtained and the tool that produced it.
  • Raw data is published alongside, so numbers can be checked rather than believed.
  • Each method runs against a reference case first — a method that does not fail where it should is not measuring anything.
  • Statements about third-party products come exclusively from publicly declared data: manifests, listing descriptions, public APIs, and the providers' own words. Retrieval date always given.
  • Nothing has been decompiled. No claim is made about any provider's intentions or about data actually transmitted — only about what is publicly declared.

What this site does not provide

Nothing here is legal, security, tax or financial advice. Measurements are single runs on specific inputs on a specific day — reports, not studies. Software changes continuously; a figure from August 2026 may be wrong three months later. Verify against the live source before relying on anything.

In particular: a PDF produced by screen capture is not a qualified electronic document. It carries no qualified signature, no trusted timestamp and no chain of custody. It records what a browser displayed at a moment in time — suitable for your own records, not equivalent to notarised or court-grade preservation of evidence.

No liability is accepted for decisions taken on the basis of content published here, to the extent permitted by law. Statutory liability for intent and gross negligence remains unaffected.

Articles on this site occasionally touch on questions with a legal dimension — permissions, licences, copyright, the evidentiary weight of a record. None of it is legal advice. For a specific situation, qualified advice is the right route.

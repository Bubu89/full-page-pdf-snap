# Chrome Web Store — Listing-Texte (EN)

Einzige Quelle für den Store-Text. Nach der Ablehnung vom 02.08.2026
("Red Nickel — Impersonation Assets") bewusst frei von Badge-Optik und den
von Google beanstandeten Schlagworten.

## Was Google beanstandet hat

> Mimicking ranking, performance, current status or promotional information:
> **free badge in media**. Extension assets should not use badge or text with
> keywords such as 'recommended', 'premium', 'free', '#1', 'new' etc.

Das galt dem grünen `FREE`-Pill in `04_free_en.png` und `promo_tile_440x280.png`.
Der Beschreibungstext trug dasselbe Muster in Textform: `++++`-Banner um
"Completely free" und die Zwischenüberschrift "FREE — AND BUILT TO STAY THAT WAY".

**Regel für alle künftigen Texte:** Kein Rahmen aus Sonderzeichen, keine
Versalien-Schlagzeilen, und die Wörter *free*, *premium*, *new*, *#1*,
*recommended*, *best* nicht als Werbeaussage. Sachliche Verneinungen
("no ads", "no account") und die Lizenzangabe ("MIT license") sind zulässig —
sie beschreiben Eigenschaften, nicht einen Store-Status.

## Description

```
Save any webpage as a single high-resolution PDF. Auto-scrolls the whole page — no cropping, no print dialog.

Full Page PDF Snap scrolls the page, captures every viewport and stitches all segments into one seamless, high-resolution PDF on your device.

HOW IT WORKS

Click the toolbar button, use the context menu or press Alt+Shift+Y. The extension scrolls to the bottom, waits for lazy-loaded images, captures each viewport and assembles everything into one continuous PDF page — no seams, no page breaks cutting through your content.

WHAT YOU GET

• One continuous page instead of a print job chopped into A4 sheets
• Resolution up to 2.0x for sharp text, ideal for OCR and AI processing
• Filename templates with site, date, time and counter
• Optional multi-page output when you actually want to print
• Cookie banners and sticky headers hidden before capture

AVAILABLE IN 9 LANGUAGES

The entire interface — popup, settings and every message — follows your browser
language automatically. No setting to change:

English · Deutsch · Español · Français · Italiano · 日本語 · Português (BR) ·
Русский · 简体中文

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

• No ads — not in the popup, not in the settings, not in your PDF
• No watermark on the output, at any resolution
• No page limit and no daily quota
• No account, no email address, no sign-up
• No subscription and no paid tier holding features back
• No upload — the page never leaves your computer
• No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking

OPEN SOURCE

PERMISSIONS

The extension asks only for what the capture needs. It has no access to your

SUPPORT

Questions, bug reports and feature requests:
```
